About the Project

Welcome to Svenska fjällklätterprojektet (which loosely translates as The Swedish Mountain Climbing Project), which aims to popularize climbing in the Swedish mountains by documenting and publishing information about climbing routes and areas (many of which are, unfortunately, all but forgotten, or at the very least it's hard to find relevant and up-to-date information about them). The main focus of the project is on alpine climbing, but there are no hard rules and anything from bouldering and sport climbing to scrambling and via ferratas might make it into the mix.

With the exception of the project's Instagram profile (where most captions are written in English) most of our published material is in Swedish. There are currently no plans to provide more information in English, but hopefully the language barrier can be bridged by modern translation software for our non-Swedish speaking friends.

If you have any questions or wish to participate in the project, don't hesitate to reach out to fjallklatterprojektet@gmail.com.
