About the Project

Welcome to Svenska fjällklätterprojektet (which loosely translates as The Swedish Mountain Climbing Project), a project that aims to popularize climbing in the Swedish mountains by documenting and publishing information about climbing routes and areas (many of which are, unfortunately, all but forgotten, or at the very least it's hard to find relevant and up-to-date information about them). The main focus of the project is on alpine climbing, but there are no hard rules and anything from bouldering and sport climbing to scrambling and via ferratas might make it into the mix.

With the exception of the project's Instagram profile (where most post captions are written in English) and short summaries of some trip reports posted over at Peakbook, most of the project's published material is written in Swedish. For non-Swedish speakers who still want to access the full trip reports and articles on this site, the language barrier can hopefully be bridged by modern translation software.

If you have any questions or wish to participate in the project, don't hesitate to reach out to fjallklatterprojektet@gmail.com.
