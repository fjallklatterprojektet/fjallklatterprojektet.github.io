# Solo traverse across the four 2000 meter peaks of Sarektjåkkå

Starting from my campsite in the upper reaches of Rapadalen near Skárjá/Skarja at 08, I followed Máhtujågåsj/Matujåkatj upstream and then continued northeast to the saddle about half a kilometer SSW of the summit of Svarta Spetsen. From there I hiked to the summit (easy but somewhat steep and a bit loose) where I took a short break to enjoy the view (at around 11 am) before scrambling down the northeast face (easy but loose).

I then followed the broad and undulating ridge going north toward Buchttoppen. Right before Peak 1906 there is a short section of easy scrambling but otherwise the ridge is straightforward, and I made it to the summit of Buchttoppen by 13:50.

The ridge from Buchttoppen to Sarektjåkkå Sydtoppen is easy (mostly hiking except for a short section of scrambling on the way down from Buchttoppen). I reached the summit of Sarektjåkkå Sydtoppen at 14:30, and from there I continued to follow the ridge north along easy terrain to Sarektjåkkå Stortoppen (the highest summit of the day) which I summited at 15:30.

The ridge connecting Sarektjåkkå Stortoppen with Sarektjåkkå Nordtoppen is slightly more difficult, with three steeper sections (all short, less than 10 m) that require downclimbing or rappelling. I downclimbed the first and the third one, and rappelled down the second, and stood on the summit of Sarektjåkkå Nordtoppen, the last summit of the day, at 16:40.

The hike down into Guhkesvágge/Kukkesvagge was facilitated by a large snowfield, which was icy enough to require crampons but still made the descent a breeze. I made it down to the valley by 18:20, and then continued north for a few hours under a beautiful evening sky before setting up camp near lake Suottasjjávvre/Suottasjaure.
