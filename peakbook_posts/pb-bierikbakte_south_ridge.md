# South Ridge of Bierikbákte

After a nice, lazy morning I packed my climbing gear and some snacks and left the rest of my stuff in the tent by the south shore of Bielajávrátja/Pielajauratj before heading out around 10. My objective for the day was to climb Bierikbákte/Pierikpakte via the south ridge, so I followed the faint trail coming down from Basstvágge/Pastavagge for a couple of kilometers before cossing Alep Basstajåhkå/Alep Pastajåkkå and then continued up the hill toward the ravine coming down from the cirque between Bierikbákte and Nilas kam.

The ravine is a bit narrow and loose in the lower part but gets better a bit higher up. I figured the easiest way up was to follow the stream at its bottom, and so I did for about 45 minutes, at which point the ravine was more like a shallow gully, and I went up its right side to gain the cirque more quickly.

Once in the cirque I continued straight up to the saddle between Bierikbákte and Nilas kam, which I gained at around 12:30. From there the south ridge of Bierikbákte starts off easy for a couple hundred meters before the steep wall that is the technical crux of the route begins a bit above 1600 m. The crux is a pitch of grade 3-4 climbing, which requires a bit of care since the rock is loose in places. I picked a line through the crux that started quite far to the right and then continued diagonally up to the right before going around a corner and gaining the slope above the crux wall.

I then stayed to the right of the ridge until right before the prominent snow-filled gully coming up from the glacier, where I crossed over to the left side of the ridge before reaching the broad, gentle slope that leads almost all the way to the summit. Where the slope ends the ridge then suddenly becomes very narrow and exposed, and I had to bust out some scrambling moves before summiting at 13:20.

The way down to the saddle I followed the exact same route that I came up. With my 30 meter rope I needed two rappels to get past the steep crux section, and I found reasonably good rocks to rappel from so no gear was left behind.

After making it down to the saddle I left my backpack there and hiked up the north west ridge of nearby Nilas kam to take some pictures of the route I had just climbed. I summited Nilas kam at 15:40, and then went down to pick up my backpack at the saddle before descending back down the cirque and the ravine. I was back at my campsite around 18:45.
